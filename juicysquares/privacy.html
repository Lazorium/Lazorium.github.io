Privacy Policy — Juicy Squares: Fruit Merge

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Last updated: 22 October 2026

App: Juicy Squares: Fruit Merge (com.lazorium.juicysquares) · Developer: Lazorium

This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and message.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • Your progress is stored on your own device.
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay and diagnostic statistics and we show ads. What our analytics and advertising providers receive is described below.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. The game works the same whatever you choose.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

1. Gameplay analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, so we can fix problems and balance difficulty.

DataDetails
Player and device identifiersOn first launch the game creates a random player identifier, stores it in your local save and passes it to GameAnalytics so that sessions and events can be grouped. It is not your device ID, your advertising ID or an account. The GameAnalytics SDK also creates or reads its own SDK identifier; on iOS its default uses Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV). Depending on platform and consent, the SDK may also receive an advertising identifier.
Gameplay and interaction eventsGame runs started, finished or abandoned (mode, score, largest fruit reached, whether boosters were used); Gold earned and spent; boosters bought; shop opened; purchase and restore outcomes (which product and whether it succeeded); ad requests, loads, impressions, rewards and failures; banner shown or hidden; daily gift claims; the result of the consent step. These events do not upload your board, your album or your full saved-game history.
Technical contextApp version, platform, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address by the provider), session start and end times.

Gameplay analytics starts only after the consent step described under Your choices. Where the GDPR applies, it runs only if you consent to storing and accessing information on your device. The provider can associate events with the player or SDK identifier, so we describe them as pseudonymous rather than anonymous. The game does not ask for your real name or create a game account.

2. Advertising

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google processes ad requests, impressions, clicks, device identifiers and diagnostic information. Google's Mobile Ads SDK can also infer approximate location from your IP address. The game does not request GPS or precise location.

  • With your consent, where consent is required, ads may be personalised.
  • Without consent, the game shows non-personalised or limited ads, chosen from context rather than from an advertising profile.
  • Players with an active No Ads subscription or No Ads Plus period see no banner or interstitial ads. Rewarded videos that you choose to watch stay available.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad. After the consent step and before ads start, iOS asks whether Juicy Squares may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you allow it can your advertising identifier (IDFA) be used: Google's SDK may then use it for personalised advertising and ad measurement, and our analytics provider may receive it. If you ask the app not to track, the identifier is not available and ads are not personalised with it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

Optional purchases (Gold packs, booster packs, the Starter Pack, the No Ads subscription and the No Ads Plus pack) are sold and billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your card number, billing address or any payment credential. The store tells the game which purchases you own so it can unlock them. The store manages renewal, cancellation and refunds.

4. Data stored on your device

Your progress, the current board, album, Gold, boosters, settings, purchase records and the game's analytics identifier are saved in the app's own storage on your device. The game does not upload this save and has no online account or cloud save of its own; your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings. Deleting the app deletes the save. Your consent choices are kept on the device by Google's consent tool. Purchases stay with your store account and can be restored.

Your choices

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

On first launch the game shows a consent form provided by Google. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners individually. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown when the requirement applies to you). If you refuse, or do not allow storing and accessing information on your device, gameplay analytics is switched off, and withdrawing that consent later switches it off again. Ads may still be shown in a non-personalised or limited form.

Everywhere else

Gameplay analytics and advertising run by default. You still have control:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: your answer to the tracking request can be changed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Apple Advertising controls Apple's own personalised ads.
  • You can email us at any time about your privacy rights or data deletion — see Your rights.

How long we keep data

Gameplay analytics is kept only as long as it is useful for the purposes above, and is then deleted or aggregated into statistics that can no longer be linked to a single player. Data stored on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

The game is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions have the right to know what is collected and to request deletion, without being treated differently for exercising it.

To exercise a right, email lazoriumgames@gmail.com. Because the game has no account and does not show its analytics identifier, we may be unable to locate a specific analytics record from an email address alone. We will explain what information is needed to identify the applicable records and any limitations of the request. Local progress can be removed by deleting the app; the store manages purchase and subscription records and cancellation separately. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
