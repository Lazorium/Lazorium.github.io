Privacy Policy — VoxelBloom

Effective date: 16 September 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

App: VoxelBloom (com.lazorium.voxelbloom) · Developer: Lazorium

This policy explains what VoxelBloom collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and message.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • Your progress is stored on your own device.
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay and diagnostic statistics and we show ads. The data collected by our analytics and advertising providers is described below.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. The game works the same whatever you choose.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

1. Gameplay analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, so we can fix problems and balance difficulty.

The analytics provider can associate events with an SDK player or device identifier, so we describe them as pseudonymous rather than anonymous. VoxelBloom does not ask you to create a game account or provide your real name.

2. Advertising

The game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google processes ad requests, impressions, clicks and your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS).

  • With your consent, where consent is required, ads may be personalised: the advertising identifier may be used to select ads based on your interests.
  • Without consent, the game shows non-personalised or limited ads instead. Ads do not disappear, but they are chosen from context rather than from an advertising profile.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK can also collect approximate location inferred from your IP address, app and ad interactions, device identifiers, and diagnostic and performance information. These data support advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and service operation. The game does not request GPS or precise location.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad. Before ads start, iOS asks whether VoxelBloom may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you allow it can Google's SDK receive your advertising identifier (IDFA) and use it for personalised advertising and ad measurement. If you ask the app not to track, the identifier is not available and ads are not personalised with it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium subscribers see no ads, and the game does not start advertising for them.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

The optional VoxelBloom Premium subscription is sold and billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your card number, billing address or any payment credential. The store tells the game whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock Premium. The store manages renewal, cancellation and refunds.

4. Reminders

With your permission, the game schedules local notifications on your device to remind you about unfinished coloring. They are created by the game on the device; no push server or notification token is used. You can turn them off in your device's notification settings.

5. Data stored only on your device

Your coloring progress, Creative drawings, settings and models unlocked by rewarded ads are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded and there is no cloud backup. Deleting the app deletes that save. Your subscription is not stored only on the device: it stays with your store account and can be restored.

Your choices

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

On first launch the game shows a consent form provided by Google. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners individually, and refusing is as easy as accepting. You can change your answer at any time in the game settings, under the ad privacy options. If you refuse, or do not allow storing and accessing information on your device, gameplay analytics is switched off; ads may still be shown in a non-personalised or limited form.

Everywhere else

Gameplay analytics and advertising run by default. You still have control:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: your answer to the tracking request can be changed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Apple Advertising controls Apple's own personalised ads.
  • You can email us at any time about your privacy rights or data deletion — see Your rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the advertising described above. Our providers may process data outside your country and rely on their own safeguards for those transfers.

How long we keep data

Gameplay analytics is kept only as long as it is useful for the purposes above, and is then deleted or aggregated into statistics that can no longer be linked to a single player. Data stored on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

VoxelBloom is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions have the right to know what is collected and to request deletion, without being treated differently for exercising it.

To exercise a right, email lazoriumgames@gmail.com. Because VoxelBloom has no game account and does not currently show the analytics SDK's player identifier in the game, we may be unable to locate a specific analytics record from an email address alone. We will explain what information is needed to identify the applicable records and any limitations of the request. Local progress can be removed by deleting the app; the store manages subscription records and cancellation separately. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
