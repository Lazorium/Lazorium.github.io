Privacy Policy — Detective Tile

Effective date: 16 September 2026 · Last updated: 16 September 2026

App: Detective Tile (com.lazorium.detectivetile) · Developer: Lazorium

This policy explains what Detective Tile collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • Your progress is stored on your own device.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and we show ads. Both are described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

1. Gameplay analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, so we can fix problems and balance difficulty.

DataDetails
A random player identifier created inside the gameGenerated on first launch and stored in your local save. It is not your device ID, not your advertising ID, and not an account.
Gameplay eventsLevel started, completed or failed; progress through cases; in-game gold earned and spent; screens opened; boosters used.
Technical contextApp version, platform, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address by the provider), session start and end times.
Error reportsTechnical details of a crash or an error inside the game.

These events describe what happens in the game. They are not used to build an advertising profile about you and are not combined with your real-world identity.

2. Advertising

The game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google processes ad requests, impressions, clicks and your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS).

  • With your consent, where consent is required, ads may be personalised: the advertising identifier may be used to select ads based on your interests.
  • Without consent, the game shows non-personalised or limited ads instead. Ads do not disappear, but they are chosen from context rather than from an advertising profile.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your card number, billing address or any payment credential. The store returns a purchase confirmation so the game can unlock what you bought.

4. Data stored only on your device

Your progress, settings and purchase entitlements are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes that save.

Your choices

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

On first launch the game shows a consent form provided by Google. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners individually, and refusing is as easy as accepting. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options. If you refuse, ads become non-personalised and gameplay analytics is switched off.

Everywhere else

Gameplay analytics and advertising run by default. You still have control:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls whether apps may ask to track you, and Apple Advertising controls personalised ads.
  • You can email us at any time to ask for your gameplay data to be deleted — see Contact.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the advertising described above. Our providers may process data outside your country and rely on their own safeguards for those transfers.

How long we keep data

Gameplay analytics is kept only as long as it is useful for the purposes above, and is then deleted or aggregated into statistics that can no longer be linked to a single player. Data stored on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

Detective Tile is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions have the right to know what is collected and to request deletion, without being treated differently for exercising it.

To exercise a right, email us. Because the game has no accounts, we may need the in-game player identifier to find your data; without it we may be unable to identify which records are yours. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
