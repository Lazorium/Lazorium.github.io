Privacy Policy — Detective Tile

Effective date: 16 September 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

App: Detective Tile (com.lazorium.detectivetile) · Developer: Lazorium

This policy explains what Detective Tile collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • Your progress is stored on your own device.
  • We collect pseudonymous gameplay and diagnostic statistics and show ads, as described below.
  • On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for tracking permission when eligible. Your choice does not block gameplay.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

1. Gameplay analytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, so we can fix problems and balance difficulty.

DataDetails
Player and SDK identifiersA random player identifier is created on first launch, saved locally and passed to GameAnalytics. It is not a game account. The SDK also uses its own identifier; on iOS its default identifier is IDFV. Depending on platform and permission, the provider may also receive an advertising identifier.
Gameplay eventsLevels started, completed or failed; progress through cases and challenges; in-game Gold earned and spent; screens opened; boosters used; purchase, restore and advertising outcomes. We do not upload your complete saved game or personal files.
Technical contextApp version, platform, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address by the provider), session start and end times.
Error reportsTechnical details of a crash or an error inside the game.

These events can be associated with the player or SDK identifier, so we describe them as pseudonymous, not anonymous. Gameplay analytics is held until the privacy check resolves. Where GDPR applies, it requires the relevant device-storage, measurement and GameAnalytics vendor consents; withdrawing those permissions stops subsequent event submission. The game does not ask for your real-world identity.

2. Advertising

The game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google processes ad requests, impressions, clicks, device identifiers and diagnostic information, and may infer approximate location from your IP address. The game does not request GPS or precise location. On iOS, IDFA is available only if you authorize tracking.

  • With your consent, where consent is required, ads may be personalised: the advertising identifier may be used to select ads based on your interests.
  • Without consent, non-personalised or limited ads may remain available where the network permits them. Refusal does not prevent you from playing.
  • Active No Ads benefits remove banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain available.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

App Tracking Transparency. On iPhone and iPad, after Google’s privacy check (and its consent form, where one is required) and before advertising starts, the game requests Apple’s tracking authorization if the status is undetermined, ads may run and iOS permits the prompt. If you decline, IDFA stays unavailable and the game requests non-personalised ads. An existing or restricted status does not trigger another prompt. Change this choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your card number, billing address or any payment credential. The store returns a purchase confirmation, product and transaction identifiers, and subscription status so the game can deliver purchases and restore eligible rights. Unity IAP passes those transactions between the game and Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by the billing store. Deleting the game does not cancel a subscription.

4. Data stored only on your device

Your progress, settings and purchase entitlements are saved in the app's own storage on your device. The game has no account or cloud-save service of its own. Your device backup may include app data, depending on its settings. Deleting the app removes its local save; eligible store entitlements may be restored with the same store account.

5. Notifications and content downloads

With notification permission, the game schedules local reminders on your device. These are not server push messages; the current game does not register a remote push token. You can disable notifications in your device settings. Later cases and the Chinese font are downloaded over HTTPS. Hosting providers, including Cloudflare, receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and the requested resource to serve these downloads; the game does not upload your progress to them.

6. Unity engine

The game uses Unity. Unity engine hardware statistics are enabled in the current build configuration and may send technical device and usage information to Unity. This is separate from the game's GameAnalytics event controls. See Unity's game-player privacy policy.

Your choices

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

Google UMP checks privacy requirements when the game starts and shows a consent form when required for your region and the configured message. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners individually. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options, shown when required by UMP. Without the relevant GameAnalytics consents, gameplay analytics is switched off. Non-personalised or limited ads may still be available.

Everywhere else

Gameplay analytics and advertising run by default. You still have control:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls whether apps may ask to track you, and Apple Advertising controls Apple’s own ads, separately from AdMob.
  • You can email us at any time to ask for your gameplay data to be deleted — see Contact.

How long we keep data

Gameplay analytics is kept only as long as it is useful for the purposes above, and is then deleted or aggregated into statistics that can no longer be linked to a single player. Data stored on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

Detective Tile is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions have the right to know what is collected and to request deletion, without being treated differently for exercising it.

To exercise a right, email us. Because the game has no accounts, we may need the in-game player identifier to find your data; without it we may be unable to identify which records are yours. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Where required, significant changes will be communicated and renewed consent requested.
